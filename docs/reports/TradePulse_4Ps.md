## 1) PRODUCT — what are we selling?

### In plain English
Banks and trade houses still finance and clear paper on **invoices + shipping docs (BoL/AWB)**. Officers open PDFs, hunt mismatches, and worry about look-alike company names and missing pages.

**TradePulse** is the **first-pass review desk**:

1. Upload the document pack  
2. System extracts facts, cross-checks documents, flags gaps/mismatches  
3. Puts counterparties on an **identity confidence ladder** (name match ≠ proof)  
4. Hands officers one **examiner case pack** for maker–checker  

### What the buyer gets
- Faster desk throughput  
- Fewer “false all-clears” when data is missing  
- A cleaner audit trail  
- Decision-support — **not** robot approval  

### What we are NOT (say this once)
- Not Customs / ICEGATE / container inspection  
- Not a payment engine  
- Not “AI approved / cleared / sanctioned this deal”  
- Not an ITFS marketplace operator  

### Analogy for cold rooms
> “Think **Bloomberg Terminal for the trade examiner’s document pile** — a workbench that prepares the file. The officer still signs.”

---

## 2) PRICE — how do we get paid?

### Who pays
**Head of Trade Finance Operations** (Ops budget).  
Compliance must accept the framing: **decision support**, not autopilot.

### Commercial model (honest for a prototype stage)
| Stage | What we charge | What they get |
|-------|----------------|---------------|
| **Now** | Supervised **pilot** (fixed fee or waived/discounted for first GIFT IBU) | One corridor, invoice + BoL, labelled data rules, human-in-the-loop |
| **Next** | **SaaS**: annual platform + **per-case** (or monthly case pack) | Seats for examiners + usage aligned to volume |
| **Later** | Enterprise contract (SSO, VPC, SLA, list adapters) | Production residency / private deploy path |

### Why this pricing logic
- Value tracks **cases processed**, not “AI magic”  
- Banks hate pure seat-tax when volume spikes — hybrid **platform + usage** matches desk economics  
- Comparable trade-doc tools sell Starter→Enterprise tiers; bank-grade tools land as **custom annual** after pilot  

### What we never sell
We do **not** charge for “approval rate” or “AI cleared the trade.” That framing is toxic for regulated buyers.

### Pitch-safe price line (no invented INR)
> “We sell a **pilot**, then **software subscription + per case**. Ops pays for **faster, cleaner files** — not for automated yes/no.”

## 3) PLACE — where does the product live / get sold?

### Channel (where the customer is)
1. **Primary beachhead:** GIFT City **IBU** trade-finance operations desks  
2. **Secondary:** mid-size private-bank / trade-house desks with IFSC corridor volume  
3. **Not day-one:** retail exporters, Customs brokers, “anyone with a PDF”

### Delivery (where the software sits)
- **Cloud workbench** (live demo today on **GCP Cloud Run**, Mumbai region `asia-south1`)  
- Officers use a browser — create case → upload → review → download handoff pack  
- Future: private network / bank residency after model-risk and security review  

### Distribution motion
> Mentor / IFIH intro → Ops champion → Compliance gate → **4–6 week supervised pilot** → expand corridor  

**Place is not an app store.** It’s a **relationship sale into a regulated desk**.

## Q&A cheat for 4 Ps

**“Is this a bank product?”**  
No — software for the bank’s **review desk**. The bank still owns the decision.

**“What’s the unit of value?”**  
A **case** (one documentary pack reviewed with an examiner handoff).

**“Why GIFT first?”**  
Concentrated IFSC trade volume, clear Ops buyer, path to supervised pilots via IFIH/mentors.

**“What’s your CAC channel?”**  
High-trust intros + live proof. Not paid acquisition.
